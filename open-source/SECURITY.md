## 私下报告 / Private reports

请通过 [官网联系入口](https://shennian.net/#contact) 的企业微信联系团队人工，先说明需要安全报告渠道。不要将漏洞内容发到官方交流群、网页客服 AI、公开 Issue 或 PR。商务 / 安全邮箱尚未确认，不猜测邮件地址。

Contact a team member privately through WeCom at the [website contact entry](https://shennian.net/#contact) and ask for a private security-reporting channel. Do not send vulnerability details to public rooms, the website support AI, public issues, or PRs. No email address is advertised until confirmed.

仅提供必要的版本、受影响范围、脱敏复现和影响；不要提供真实密钥、数据库、用户日志或私人资料。没有授权时，不对他人或正式实例实施探测、利用或破坏。泄露凭据应首先撤销 / 轮换，不能仅删文件后认为风险消失。

Provide minimal versions, affected scope, sanitized reproduction, and impact. Do not include real keys, databases, user logs, or private content. Do not probe or exploit systems without authorization. Revoke or rotate leaked credentials; deleting a file alone does not remediate exposure.
